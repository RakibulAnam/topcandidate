# Content guide — audience & hooks

## Who's watching

Bangladeshi job seekers — students, fresh grads, early/mid-career — scrolling Facebook,
Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, mostly in Banglish. What they feel:

- applying to dozens of jobs (Bdjobs, LinkedIn, Facebook groups) and hearing nothing back
- feeling invisible to recruiters; not knowing *why* they're rejected
- CV insecurity ("amar CV ki thik ase?"), copying templates, paying a CV writer
- interview anxiety, especially in English
- salary pressure, family expectations, competition, fear of missing the "right" circular
- LinkedIn confusion (what to write, who to message)

The product's answer: **one paste → a complete application written for *that* job.** Strong
videos make the viewer recognise their own mistake, then show the fix.

## Hook system

Pick one angle per video. Say it in the first sentence; show it in the first frame.

| Angle | Pattern | Banglish example (adapt — don't reuse verbatim) |
|---|---|---|
| Contrarian | "X isn't the problem. Y is." | "Apnar CV kharap na. Problem hocche…" |
| Negative / mistake | "Stop doing X." | "Shob job-e same CV pathano bondho korun." |
| Curiosity | open loop | "Recruiter apnar CV-r prothom line-ei ki dekhe jaanen?" |
| Fear / stakes | cost of inaction | "Ei ek bhul-er jonno apni interview call pacchen na." |
| Frustration | name the pain exactly | "50 ta apply, 0 ta call. Sound familiar?" |
| Disagreement | challenge common advice | "'CV ek page-er hote hobe' — shob shomoy na." |
| Surprise / number | concrete figure | "৳200-e 5 ta full application." (true — see BRAND) |
| Status | insider knowledge | "HR-ra je jinish ta kokhono bole na…" |
| Financial | money angle | "CV writer-ke ৳3,000 deyar age eta dekhen." (price range is from the landing page) |
| Career mistake | specific error | "Cover letter-e 'Dear Sir/Madam' likhe shuru korsen?" |

Rules: hooks must be **true and specific**; no fake stats, no invented recruiter quotes, no "AI".
Don't promise outcomes the product doesn't (jobs, interviews, salary).

## Script shapes that work

- **Pain → reframe → why → fix → product → CTA** (templates/job-seeker-pain.json)
- **Mistake → what it looks like → better version** (before-after)
- **Question → answer in 3 beats → product as the shortcut** (talking-head)
- **Meme setup → job-seeker twist → TopCandidate punchline** (meme)

## Tone

Like a sharp senior friend who's done hiring: direct, a bit provocative, warm, never preachy,
never corporate. Banglish as people actually speak it.
